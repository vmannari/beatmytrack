# Research notes and evidence boundaries

Research informs experiments; it does not overrule the user's taste.

## Expectation and surprise
Cheung et al., *Current Biology* (2019) found musical pleasure related to interactions between uncertainty and surprise in harmonic sequences. Useful implication: establish expectation, then vary it; do not interpret this as a fixed composition formula.
https://www.cell.com/current-biology/fulltext/S0960-9822(19)31050-7

## Groove and syncopation
Witek et al., *PLOS ONE* (2014) found an inverted-U relationship in their funk-drum stimuli: moderate syncopation produced stronger groove/pleasure responses than very low or very high syncopation. Useful implication: rhythmic interest can coexist with stability.
https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0094446

## Driving-game music
Cassidy & MacDonald (2009) reported differences in driving-game performance/experience depending on music condition; high-arousal experimenter-selected music was not automatically beneficial. Useful implication: do not equate intensity with better driving experience.
https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/102986490901300207

## Adaptive music
Hutchings & McCormack, *IEEE Transactions on Games*, studied adaptive music generation and reported mixed evidence. Useful implication: adaptivity does not compensate for weak underlying music.
https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01154

## Modular authored music
Koji Kondo described Hyrule Field music in *Ocarina of Time* as multiple small authored components designed to connect in different orders. Useful implication: modularity benefits from deliberate transition-aware composition.
https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Iwata-Asks/Iwata-Asks-The-Legend-of-Zelda-Ocarina-of-Time-3D/Vol-1-Sound/2-Koji-Kondo-Upends-the-Tea-Table/2-Koji-Kondo-Upends-the-Tea-Table-231296.html

## Game-score practice
Useful case-study principles:
- recurring identity can transform with context (Ori, Journey)
- strong audiovisual aesthetic coherence matters (Hotline Miami)
- unusual instrumentation is strongest when it belongs to the world/role (Outer Wilds, Hades)
- musical structure can directly reinforce mechanics (Crypt of the NecroDancer), but Beat my track should not force steering/braking to a beat

These are qualitative design references, not scientific proof of superiority.

## Evidence language
Use:
- “suggests”
- “supports testing”
- “documented practice”
- “in this study/sample”

Avoid:
- “proves this is optimal”
- “dopamine formula”
- “scientifically best soundtrack”
