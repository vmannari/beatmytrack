# Financial model / visual-scope reconciliation

Revision 2 updates the approved visual direction, camera, UI, technical handoff and roadmap. `Financial_model.xlsx` is preserved byte-for-byte from v1: no conversion, pricing, install, retention, cost or profit assumption was changed to make the visual concept look more attractive. Its old visual assumptions are no longer a complete production estimate.

## Provisional added planning allowance

P0-V adds 2–4 productive person-weeks for a one-car / one-corner visual slice, prototype race/garage composition and device profiling. At the existing 30 productive hours/week planning convention this is 60–120 direct hours, or 75–150 hours including a 25% buffer. These are proposed planning allowances, not quotes or measured production throughput.

The corresponding complete roadmap arithmetic changes from 20–34 to 22–38 direct person-weeks, or from 25–42.5 to 27.5–47.5 with that buffer. This does NOT cover a reliably estimated six-car / twelve-track AAA asset effort. Car modelling, material authoring, modular environment production, technical art, sound polish and optimization must be re-estimated after P0-V.

At an owner-selected opportunity-cost rate R in EUR/hour, the incremental buffered slice labour is 75R–150R. Keep that distinct from cash spending. With existing hardware/free tools and self-produced art, new software licence expense can remain zero, while the owner still spends time. Outsourcing, asset purchases, extra test devices and paid AI usage are optional cash assumptions requiring approval. Online service/moderation costs from the existing game scope remain; attractive graphics do not remove them.

## Before treating the workbook as a revised release budget

Measure hours to finish one car/corner; record reuse ratios; estimate remaining cars/tracks; include phone optimization and QA. Then deliberately update the workbook's development/prelaunch labour/cash assumptions and rerun the cash/profit scenarios. Do not increase conversion or projected sales simply because the art direction is improved. No financial forecast or new monetization terms are approved in this revision.
