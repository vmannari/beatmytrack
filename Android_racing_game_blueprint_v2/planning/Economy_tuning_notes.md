# Economy table interpretation

The CSV is an illustrative installation-cost curve, not production content or a store price list. Stock is tier 0; four earned upgrades per system. Total across the five example systems is 135,000 credits. At 4 credits per eligible driving second plus a 10% valid-completion bonus, the uninterrupted theoretical rate is 15,840 credits/hour, so full installation spend is roughly 8.5 eligible driving hours before other rewards/spending. Actual wall-clock progression differs because of menus, incomplete runs, eligibility, XP gates and car purchases. This illustrates the master specification's proposed 8–12-hour core-development target; it does not validate it.

The UI's 3,000-credit gearbox is a sample interaction rather than frozen content. Reconcile UI/content tables when implementing P2. Determine the XP-to-level curve from a ten-hour simulated session mix and phone playtests; do not invent XP gates that contradict the cost target. Small first upgrades can happen within 5–10 minutes; a few fun initial minutes should precede asking the player to understand five upgrade systems. Cosmetic prestige can continue after finite power upgrades. No daily base-reward reduction and no paid workaround for slow progress.
