01

Free Race

Pick a circuit and chase your personal best over five laps. Restart and try again whenever you like.

02

Weekly Rivalry

Take on a weekly selection of tracks with drivers of similar pace. Your best verified results count.

03

Challenges

Set a time and share an invitation for a one-to-one or group challenge on the same track and car.

Local racing works without an online account. Online competitions and sharing need sign-in, an internet connection and an available game service.

02 / Create

That corner
in your head?
Build it.

Lay out roads, shape the surroundings and place the start line. Go from an idea to a circuit you can actually drive.

  • Combine straights, curves, hairpins and bridges.
  • Choose surfaces and add trackside objects.
  • Test your layout in untimed practice.
  • Keep local drafts or publish a validated track for others.

03 / Garage

Make it
your own.

Explore cars with distinct handling, from the nimble Compact to the Kart, Formula, Electric and even a rear-steering Forklift.

Try paint, patterns, stickers and cosmetic parts in the Garage. Take a parking-hall test drive, or wash off the dirt from your last outing.

Cosmetic options can require unlocks. Previewing a look does not purchase it.

Deletion permanently removes your online account and remaining online benefits, including paid benefits.
