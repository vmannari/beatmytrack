# Bridge downhill release - 2026-10-17

The owner reports that descending from the bridge stays glued to the road and does not match the entry hop. The previous release explicitly required positive rising support, so it excluded the downhill crest. BD01 adds a shallow downhill tangent release, BD02 covers landing feedback, and BD03 preserves entry, replay/rewind and version isolation.

The final change gives the car a brief lift as the descending deck falls away. It adds no upward kick. Fast controlled descents across all five cars measure 9.5-21.2 cm of clearance and 0.158-0.267 seconds in the air; the Compact's 24 m/s standard-span pass measures about 16.8 cm. Slow 3 m/s crossings remain planted. Original entry snapshots are exact in both directions for all five cars.

Eight bounded candidate arcs use the shared actual axle support. A single 37-double scratch field (296 bytes) predicts every canonical tick through the short landing window, preventing an early invisible skip followed by a missed crest. The chosen constant downward acceleration fits the short game-scale ramp; it is not a claim of literal Earth-gravity simulation. Actual motion remains a world-space ballistic arc. The existing 24-tick cooldown still suppresses repeated releases at one crest, while an entry landing can no longer hide a separate downhill crest on a short span. Low-speed ground-level layer changes cannot trigger a hop.

Suspension and the existing chassis thump use that contact speed with a 2.5 m/s feedback bound, avoiding hard-impact sparks and a violent body slam from an otherwise shallow supported hop.
