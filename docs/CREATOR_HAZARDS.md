Requirements: V02/V04/V05 (consistent mature woodland art and reviewed rendering), R01/T01-T04 (local creator selection, editing, persistence and bounded data), G01 (car response), and the owner's 2026-09-14 request. This checkpoint implements tapered kerbs, solid/deletable trees, matching bridge asphalt and scalable objects with deterministic hazard effects.

## Behavior

- Kerb runs retain complete equal alternating blocks measured along the strip. A second UV coordinate narrows the first and last white block into the edge line. Taper length is the smaller of a complete block and 1.8 m. No extra gravel band is added behind kerbs; the existing rough shoulder and grass blend remain.
- Objects > Select selects an authored or automatic woodland tree. Automatic trees become ordinary document objects on first selection. Delete, drag, rotate, copy, Size -/+, undo/redo and saving then use the existing editor flow. `hidden_scenery` suppresses the original site after deletion or moving. Blank maps start without automatic trees; original woodland road documents have at most 48 deterministic sites, with road/deck/start clearance. Tiny shrubs and pebbles remain decorative. Unedited native fixtures no longer draw decorative non-solid pine trees; their remixes preserve the original cells, and trees placed through Objects are solid and editable.
- Added Boulder, Slate and Cliff alongside Rock. Their footprints scale with the meshes and remain solid. Standard tree canopies now fit their 3 m nominal footprint. Fixed seed placement is spread across the map; generation and collision stamping happen in the compiler, not in the canonical simulation step.
- Size controls support 0.5-3x in 0.25 increments for objects other than loose tyre stacks. Tyre stacks keep their existing physical dimensions; extend their line for a longer wall. Rotation and copy preserve scale. Cliffs are freestanding rock walls, not terrain-height editing.
- Oil is a dark irregular spill: tyre-grip factor 0.20. Shallow water is a rippled patch: grip factor 0.45 plus speed-dependent drag, in addition to the native shallow-water surface response. These effects apply on the ground, not on bridge decks or in flight. Pond retains the existing deep-water blocking behavior.
- Jump is a directional 6 x 5 m ramp, 1 m high at standard size. Its arrow points toward the high lip. Crossing that lip in the arrow direction at 5 m/s or faster starts a ballistic arc; gravity is 9.81 m/s^2, initial vertical speed is `min(8, 2 + planar_speed * 0.20)`. A 120-tick cooldown prevents repeat triggering. Airborne tyre steering/drive/brake forces are inactive; landing retains 88% of planar speed. The visual car climbs the ramp and pitches with flight velocity. Airborne skid marks are suppressed.
- Bridge decks use the shared procedural asphalt detail and restrained grey guardrails on discrete posts. Correct outward rock/branch winding removes inverted-hull striping. Spill contours and effects share the same radial footprint function. All meshes/shaders in this slice are original code assets; the existing grass asset's provenance remains in PAINTED_GRASS_PROMPT.md.
