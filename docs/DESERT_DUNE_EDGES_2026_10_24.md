## Result and implementation

Sand births use 14 authored crest guides in the existing `desert_sand_v2.png`. Each guide supplies eight birth coordinates. The broad, high-contrast edges are traced within artist-selected windows; the softly lit lower crowns use pinned lip coordinates so the tracer cannot jump onto an unrelated ripple. The atlas is data generated by `scripts/bake_desert_dune_edges.py`; the PNG is unchanged, SHA256 `716bddbf85086fa1d226ac0b58a44d14418b85d656fe6dcfe9f3776696df95aa`.

Use the visible albedo's exact **world XZ / 28m** coordinates, including negative-world wrapping. The unrelated 32m normal-map tile does not position emitters. Every MultiMesh instance starts at its own traced root. Remove the original lateral birth jitter; cards start narrow and nearly touching the ground, widen after lift, then drift downwind. Even age spacing and a per-layer opacity cap keep coincident root cards from building hard cores. Airborne hue, low lift and depth/roof exclusion remain.

Ground support comes from the actual terrain floor and shared Desert relief. A conservative occupied-cell index excludes the raised banks whose shader replaces the photo with vertex pigment; a loose-surface margin avoids the road fringe. It scans the prepared geometry once and coalesces its projection rather than retaining all subdivision triangles. The native fixture reads 84,680 input triangles into 1,671 occupied cells. The cell map is capped at 65,536 entries and coarsens conservatively if needed. A local 3×3 tile window follows the car, so the anchor cap does not fill with one end of a large map. At most 126 new sites/1,008 root probes are considered per refresh, plus up to six retained live sites.

Entire sites fade by their source distance before releasing their slot. CPU retirement and the shader share the same 14–20m source fade. The visible-site stability test now references that shared constant (still 20m); its guarantee is preserved. No arbitrary flat-ground fallback remains: all emitted roots come from the artwork guides. Empty or covered guide locations stay empty.

Independent periods change **5–9s → 3.6–6s**, retaining the 10% skipped-event variation. This is about **46% more nominal starts** at a site, inferred from the mean periods, not a measured device-wide start count. Durations are 2.3–3.2s, bounded to fade before a cycle rolls over. Six sites still share 48 quads/96 triangles. Wind audio, camera, terrain artwork, pyramid repair and canonical driving code are outside the runtime edit scope.
