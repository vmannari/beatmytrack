# Desert surface hierarchy — 2026-10-03

Requirements **V02/V04/V05/Q01**; presentation kit **110**. The owner supplied a desert racing reference and requested varied sand, stronger dune relief, gravelly shoulders, sparse vegetation and rocks, warm daylight and readable driving. Later corrections reject dark tire tracks and the objects' shapes/colors. The final treatment has lighter dusty-brown grooves, irregular weathered stones and dry branching scrub in quieter mineral/straw colors.

The three layers are now smooth compacted racing sand, a rough gravelly shoulder and loose dune sand. The compacted-road pigment is warmer while its approved directional grain and geometry remain. Loose sand uses the existing original dune artwork at a slightly broader 28 m repeat, with independently sampled mineral variation, brighter crests, deeper sheltered faces and stronger bounded slope lighting. The shoulder retains its separate 6 m grit artwork, with restrained deposit variation and actual low pebbles. Warm ambient shade and a modestly brighter sun retain the normal daylight angle. No reference image was copied into a shipping asset; no new texture, light, fog/overlay pass, dependency or runtime generation service was added. Original texture provenance remains in `DESERT_DUNES_PROMPT.md` and `DESERT_APRON_PROMPT.md`.

Dunes, including those inside the hairpin island, use **artwork and normal relief**, not raised driveable terrain or dynamic dune shadow geometry. This preserves the existing planar ground/contact contract. The dune artwork provides soft directional depth; low rock geometry uses the existing directional light. Existing desert driving dust continues to provide the dusty atmosphere; this task does not alter its population, lifetime or shader. Concurrent dust optimization is recorded separately in `DESERT_DUST_PERFORMANCE.md`.

Low cover now has irregular, elongated weathered stones with unequal bevels, broad worn top planes and varied dimensions. Quiet grey/beige mineral colors and soft diffuse lighting replace dark polygon beads. Sparse scrub uses thin bent stems, twig forks and narrow frayed straw bundles instead of broad star-shaped leaves. Site groups vary from one to three stones; scrub sites usually omit stones entirely. Full 1.2 m site footprints are checked against canonical roads, bridge decks and authored objects. Decorative stones stay low and passable; authored solid objects retain their existing collision. At most 480 broad cover sites and 192 uniformly distributed route stations feed the same opaque scenery batch; the small-desert-scenery outline pass is removed. Roadside work participates in existing sliced menu/editor construction.

Final saved-circuit cover contains 695 stones including 25 larger ones, 192 extra roadside pebbles and 95 scrub bushes: **39,338 triangles**, down from the rejected object's 43,434. The hairpin fixture contains 371 stones including 11 larger ones, 183 roadside pebbles and 65 scrub bushes: 25,646 triangles. These are actual geometry counts, **not a measured mobile performance result**. The pale color is carried by vertex pigment and Lambert diffuse planes, using the existing light rig; no new texture/material pass is added.

Tire grooves retain tread, faceted pressed-sand relief, width and physics-driven strength. The rejected first pass used RGBA `(0.44, 0.31, 0.17, 0.70)`; the owner-corrected version uses `(0.61, 0.48, 0.30, 0.50)`. Leaving desert restores the existing dirt palette. HUD, car/road/collision scale, gameplay camera, canonical driving, lap gates, rules and compiler versions are unchanged by this task.
