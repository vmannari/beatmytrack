# Desert bank blend, bridge continuity and warm road colour

2026-10-13 owner correction. DS18 adopts Winter's settled/granular road-to-bank transition; DS19 lightens the road and reduces wear contrast; DS20 fixes road-to-bridge pattern continuity; DS21 responds to the later rejection of the dull road hue. Preserve V02/V04/V05/G01/R01/Q01, original nine-second desert dust, approved tire-mark colour, low wind-shaped deposits, five laps, local per-car condition and canonical driving/scoring.

Winter's `winter_bank.gdshader` blends a settled deposit and broken fine grains with `shoulder_fringe`, while its actual road retains directional material detail. Desert now uses that same shared fringe, with broad world-space breakup, over a 1.95 m cosmetic foot instead of the earlier 0.93 m strip/approximately 0.28 m interpolation. The foot carries the existing compacted-road texture through the blend into the same loose shoulder material used on the bank. Bank geometry, crown height, fragments and terrain relief are unchanged.

The first lighter but still brown `847357` road was rejected during this task. The final road is restrained golden sand `9f8052`, with shared warm `ad8957` shoulders. Road and loose sand follow the dunes' warm hue; a small value difference and directional wear distinguish compaction. No scene-lighting or camera change is used to conceal the previous palette mismatch. The wear multiplier is reduced from approximately 0.90–1.08 to 0.9475–1.0375, retaining the existing original v3 artwork, mip filtering, connected phase and local junction blend. Intermediate brown and stronger golden captures are retained separately.

Ground road transverse UV is measured with `Vector2.orthogonal()`; bridge geometry uses the opposite transverse basis. The old deck buffer disagreed with the ground by 12 m at a 12 m-wide road's edges, at all four tested headings. Correcting the transverse coordinate before applying the existing phase/sign makes actual deck UV agree with the ground, including reversed spans. A desert-only Burley shader matches ground diffuse lighting. The same settled sand foot continues onto both ramp mouths and recedes over four metres, preventing a texture border along the approach shoulders. Raised bridge geometry, ramps, rails and canonical contacts are retained. Desert bridge shaders receive sampler specialization, including the retained native raster bridge path.
