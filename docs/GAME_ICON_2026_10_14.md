`game/assets/app_icon.png` is a 1024 × 1024 high-quality bicubic resize of the
1152 × 1152 source crop at (236, 124). It keeps the entire visible car and its
Garage backdrop. `game/project.godot` uses it as `application/config/icon`, and
`game/export_presets.cfg` uses it as `launcher_icons/main_192x192`.

`game/assets/app_icon_adaptive.png` is 432 × 432. Its central 288 × 288 region
has the same source framing as the square icon. The full image represents a
1728 × 1728 source area centred at (812, 700), with edge pixels extended only
where the area exceeds the supplied screenshot. This adds the Android bleed
area without clipping the car when a launcher masks the icon. The same opaque
photo is used for both adaptive layers, retaining the Garage background.
Dimensions, layering and safe-circle guidance were checked against the
[Godot 4.7 Android export documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/tutorials/export/exporting_for_android.html#providing-launcher-icons).
