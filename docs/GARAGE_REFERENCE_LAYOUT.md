The right side wall holds one uninterrupted open display alcove rather than six divided cubbies: deep returns, brushed gold trim, visible perimeter light strips, three warm spotlights, six future-award bases and handleless lower storage. An original laurel/Beat my track mural is permanent decor, not an earned trophy. The existing separate trophy camera now faces this wider display, easing in and returning to the car on submenu selection. No award ownership or grants are fabricated; the wall remains empty until future award integration.

All functional garage equipment is on the back wall: shutter and runners, a workbench/pegboard with ring/open-ended tools and screwdrivers, complete spare wheels, a red drawer cabinet, supply shelves, bottles, toolboxes, formed safety cans and a compressor. Rear wall lamps produce warm pools; roof trusses have triangular webs and suspended luminaires. The left wall has an original framed typographic poster, with restrained gold diagonal artwork. A plant and floor trim support the display corner. The room uses original batched bevelled geometry and the existing full/thumbnail PackedScene caches; it adds no imported/generated reference media, external dependency, vendor or runtime AI.

Concrete has interpolated mottling, fine grain, panel seams, controlled roughness/metallic response, warm display light wash and soft analytic reflections of the two authored roof luminaires. These reflections are a limited shader approximation of those lights, not reflected room/car geometry, SSR or ray tracing. Static furnishing contact shadows give the equipment grounding. Garage surfaces use smooth diffuse lighting while existing vehicle paint/glass/weathering remain separate.

The showroom previously rendered at logical UI dimensions and was then enlarged with the window. This is menu presentation only; the driving render scale/camera and canonical model/handling/scoring remain unchanged.
