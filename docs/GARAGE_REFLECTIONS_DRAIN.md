# Recessed garage drain and live surface reflections

Owner request, 2026-10-12. GRD01: proper floor drain; GRD02: reflections on the floor and pedestal; GRD03: bounded capture cost and correct view lifecycle. Preserve GC06–GC28/GW01–GW03/CW13/CW15/CW16/V06/G01/R01/Q01. This authorizes the requested garage surface gloss/reflections beyond the earlier restrained matte presentation; vehicle finish and gameplay rendering are unchanged.

The previous twenty-two detached bars are replaced by an original inset 0.72m-wide, 3.30m-long channel at the same location. A dark liner lies below the floor, with side/end returns, flush metal frame, support rails, three removable grate sections, sixty-nine transverse grate bars and six frame fasteners. The concrete shader opens the floor inside that frame, exposing the below-floor channel rather than laying painted bars on a solid plane. Top lips are slightly proud and grate bars nearly flush. Smooth steel was retained after inspection so fine brushing would not alias into a checker pattern at distance.

The floor and deck now sample actual rendered scene geometry through two planar reflection cameras. Both share the main showroom's World3D; there is no duplicated car, condition state or simulation. Each camera mirrors the primary camera across its receiving plane, retaining a right-handed basis and correcting the image's X direction when sampled. The floor capture includes the workshop, trophy display, car and platform base; the pedestal capture includes the actual car. Receiving surfaces and contact overlays are excluded from the source masks, preventing recursive reflection feedback. Car rotation, paint/dirt/wash changes, light sweeps, selection, tilt, zoom and trophy-camera movement therefore use the actual scene state.

Captures use half the physical main-view width, capped at 768px, matching its aspect ratio with 2x MSAA. Transparent capture backgrounds prevent empty parts of the image from darkening the entire surface. A five-tap filter softens the image, and angle-dependent weights keep concrete reflections subtler than the deck's car reflection. The floor remains textured sealed concrete; the pedestal remains satin metal with retained trim/markings. Removed the periodic floor micro-normal pattern that the new sheen amplified into moire. Existing roughness/grain/seams remain. Carousel thumbnails keep one-shot rendering and the same updated deck surface finish, without two additional live reflection cameras per card. Both captures stop on hidden pages and texture bindings are cleared on teardown.

The reflection views are a deliberate bounded presentation approximation, not ray tracing or SSR. The pedestal capture reflects the car rather than the entire room, and the floor capture does not perform a general oblique clipping-plane pass for arbitrary below-floor scenery. The dark drain liner is the only deliberately recessed decoration here. The existing soft analytic roof-light approximation remains alongside actual scene reflection samples. Main camera framing, identical platform geometry, local cosmetics, canonical car mesh/handling/scoring/PB/replay contracts and backend behavior remain unchanged.

Import passes. Turning the actual car changes the pedestal capture while preserving the real camera transform and condition. With reflections toggled off under the same pose/light, measured pedestal-region changed pixels are **14,438 Compatibility / 14,277 Mobile**; floor-region changed pixels **25,647 on each**. Tests also cover correct mirrored-camera winding/height, same-world source, capture bounds, replacement binding, hidden-page stop/resume, real inset drain and no cosmetic saves. The ordinary car remains 64 meshes/104,558 triangles/68,996 UV vertices with exact baked/source geometry and glazing bytes; no invariant assertion was removed or weakened.

Final RTX 3090 Compatibility render-work sample, 120 measured rotating frames: main 1168x372, each reflection 584x186; sum of all three per-viewport timers CPU median/p95 **1.725/2.472ms**, GPU median/p95 **0.726/1.312ms**. These sums describe captured render work, not full application wall-frame timing, isolated incremental cost or phone performance. The normal model diagnostic separately measured 488.594ms cold construction and main-view-only CPU median/p95 0.873/1.267ms, GPU 0.791/2.688ms; its layout/pixel coverage differs from the aggregate sample, so do not interpret their difference as a speedup.

Initial native cubemap probes produced broad highlights rather than sufficiently aligned car reflections and an engine texture-RID warning on one Mobile shutdown; that prototype was replaced entirely by these planar captures. Transparent backgrounds and explicit texture unbinding corrected the dark empty-surface contribution and avoided that shutdown warning in the final runs. The floor's periodic bump and fine drain brushing were removed after their moire became visible. Final captures use the revised geometry/materials and actual same-world reflection output.
