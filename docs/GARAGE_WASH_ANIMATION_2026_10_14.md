The existing closest-triangle picker now returns its original condition coordinate plus the actual world contact and outward normal. The same nearest hit still feeds the original wash operation and swipe interpolation. A small closed beveled sponge with a fabric finish and rubber back rocks against that patch. Short-lived ivory suds and pale water droplets emerge there and shrink away. Releasing stops the sponge immediately; remaining particles drain in under one second. The effect is prepared lazily on the first Wash action, outside the car model. It reuses two fixed MultiMeshes (24 suds and 32 droplets), two small sponge parts and existing native finish shaders. Four extra geometry draws per existing view are active only during feedback; no shadow casters, texture assets, car-material replacements, new viewport or reflection passes are added. The original floor/deck captures include the effect through their existing layers. Slot-index sequences avoid touching global RNG state. Turning/zooming, cancellation, leaving Care, hiding and changing the car clear transient geometry; normal teardown frees it. Cleaning rates, wash volume, local condition schema/saves, actual car/kit/livery/condition, retained room/camera and canonical driving/scoring remain unchanged.
