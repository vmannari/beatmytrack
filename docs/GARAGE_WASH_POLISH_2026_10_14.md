# Garage washing visual refinement

Owner request, 2026-10-14: improve the first washing animation toward the AAA target, followed by a specific rejection of the foam style. GWP01: a believable soft wash mitt and material finish. GWP02: visually richer connected foam. GWP03: natural scrubbing, water and runoff. GWP04: actual surface seating, bounded resources and preserved local cleaning/input/condition. GWA01-GWA03, CW13/CW15/CW16, V06/G01/R01/Q01 remain applicable.

The rectangular sponge is now an original rounded microfiber mitt with a thumb, closed nap tufts and an actual closed ribbed cuff with a stitched finish. Filtered fabric shading preserves a matte, soft appearance. The mitt gently compresses and follows the swipe direction; rapid reversals retain its orientation rather than flipping it. A held stroke uses a smaller, slower oval scrub. Water has smooth translucent dielectric droplets, a restrained Fresnel rim and gravity-shaped elongation, with lower outward velocity.

The owner's foam feedback is incorporated in the final version. It replaces separated speckled stamps and a flat blurred film with connected creamy froth, real raised small air pockets, irregular softened outlines and elongated overlapping streaks behind the mitt. The dense base avoids dark crater-like bubble holes. Tiny aeration is filtered at display scale; the rounded air pockets provide actual highlights and volume. Surface-projected gravity moves side-panel lather downward. Geometry and materials remain native and original, without a generated bitmap, external dependency or runtime service.

The original 24 lather / 32 water pools, one lazily created effect and four geometry draws per receiving view remain. Three immutable mitt/cuff/lather meshes are shared across the five cars; water reuses a smooth sphere in its fixed MultiMesh. The existing two reflection views include the effect, with no new camera or colour capture. Lather reads the current view's opaque depth once to suppress unsupported edges and floating halos. Reconstruction follows [Godot's depth documentation](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/shaders/advanced_postprocessing.html); per-instance lifetime data follows [Godot's spatial shader API](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/shaders/shader_reference/spatial_shader.html). No extra shadow caster, car material/mesh change, wash-rate change, condition schema/save change, camera change or canonical simulation change is introduced. Visual cleanup now owns a shallow copy of the hit descriptor so it cannot clear the caller's data.
