# Rally body kit refinement — 1 November 2026

The internal Compact is the Rally car. Its nine existing body choices retain their saved IDs and stock remains unchanged. The non-stock kits have individually shaped sills and front/rear valances, different arch shoulders, and adjusted flare, depth and ride-clearance profiles. Off-road flaps and competition splitters remain independently fitted. The broad flared arches and wraparound bumpers of [Peugeot's 205 Dimma](https://www.media.stellantis.com/fr-fr/peugeot/press/passion-gti-peugeot-met-son-mythique-label-gti-a-l-honneur-au-salon-retromobile) and the sharper aero of [Hyundai's i20 N Rally1](https://www.hyundaimotorgroup.com/en/story/CONT0000000000075653) informed the fictional designs; no reference models or imagery were incorporated.

The body mesh cache and both desktop-renderer thumbnail sets were regenerated, as were the 18 full/simple body modules. A body-specific module key prevents packaged builds from accepting the previous body modules. The existing kit IDs and stock geometry remain unchanged.

Validation used pinned Godot 4.7.2, isolated user data, an empty API URL, and `scripts/run_no_crash_dialog.py`. An earlier source geometry pass recorded 5,375 checks and solid construction passed 1,354; the final source and prepared attachment checks each passed 5,114; and the expanded catalogue passed 1,593 checks, including prepared mesh equivalence and 13,000 combinations at each detail level. Maximum full/simple counts were 169,376/66,420 triangles. Forty final native showroom source captures covering all body choices are in `build/rally-body-personality/final`. An initial attachment run found 175 failures; a previous-mesh control passed, and the backing was extended before the successful final runs. The first visual pass also exposed protruding bumper accents, which were removed.

The broader Compact modular all-kits test reported 91 failures in 1,263 checks, all on expected asset-hit or fallback-hit assertions. Its stock base had a different build revision from the current identity, and a concurrently edited Electric source changed after the body-module bake; the loader therefore fell back. The body modules were rebaked once more, but the full modular suite has not been rerun on a stable shared-source snapshot. Do not treat that suite as passing.

The captures and renderer tests are desktop-only. Reload and owner appearance acceptance remain, as does the normal build identity and matching prepared-asset rebuild when the concurrent source work is complete. No owner save, device, live service, deployment, or main-index write was used.
