# Star spline lap completion — 2026-10-06

Owner report: laps on **star** could not complete, suggesting incorrectly generated spline checkpoints. Requirements: G01, R01, T01–T04, V03/V05, Q01, SP08 (spline corner gates accept legitimate inside lines while rejecting skipped sections).

The report is reproduced and fixed. The generator recognized sharp joins between road pieces but overlooked sharp turns inside a smoothly fitted spline. On Star, straight gate planes beside a tight apex forced a legal inside line to double back through a small loop. A real recorded compact-car drive on the ordinary inside line completes **zero laps under the original gate generator and five under the corrected generator**, with identical car motion and no collisions. Restarting the race configures the corrected gates; no map edit is required.

## Implementation and versioning

`TrackSession._road_corners` additionally recognizes tight turns at the spline's three internal interpolation knots. It uses the adjoining authored road directions to form a bisector gate, the actual width of that knot, the timed route direction and the existing miter/reach rules. Ordinary dense gates within that corner's corridor are omitted through the existing spacing mechanism. Star changes from **32 gates / 2 recognized corners to 19 gates / 11 recognized corners**. Every remaining gate still must be crossed in order; no automatic time penalty or shortcut credit is introduced.

The implementation is deterministic and computed once when configuring a session. Car handling, physics version, road geometry/cells, spline points/widths, compiler22, start placement and visuals are unchanged. Opposite start-line direction is covered. Rules advance explicitly to **standing-5-road-gates-v19**, because checkpoint acceptance changes. Historical v18 boards are not rewritten or merged with v19 boards.

The backend's existing explicit contract-upgrade adapter now derives v19 metadata and new race tickets from retained v12–v18 immutable revisions. Metadata and tickets use the same effective contract; rivalry eligibility recognizes those retained revisions when the active verifier is v19. Stored revision manifests, track IDs/share URLs and source documents remain unchanged. New attempts use a distinct v19 board hash, leaving historical PBs on their original boards. The compatibility matrix checks ownership/private metadata and both race and creator-test tickets. No database migration or score migration is added.
