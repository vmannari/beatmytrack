# Track objects and palette refinement

Owner request, 2026-10-12: review every track object, refine the overly simple models, and improve the menu icons, with AAA as the quality target.

Requirements: TO01 — all 34 placeable families / 37 variants; TO02 — legible, coherent icons of the actual models; TO03 — unchanged canonical collision, hazards, movement, placement, undo and saves; TO04 — consistent restrained cel-shaded pigment on Compatibility and Mobile; TO05 — bounded caching and measured desktop cost; TO06 — actual captures and explicit remaining owner/device acceptance. Preserve V04-V06/G01/R01/Q01 and the existing speed-bump, continuous-barrier and winter contracts.

The original shared kit now has tapered trunks and visible branching, layered leaf masses, folded grass blades, flower petals, fractured stones/outcrops, cliff scree and log bark/end grain. Track furniture uses Jersey concrete profiles, folded guardrail sections, diamond wire mesh, timber joints, framed signs, moulded cones, shaped lamp housings and a lattice start gantry. Crates have planks/braces/nails, pallets have forklift gaps, steel drums have hoops and bungs, and containers have corrugations, corner castings, hinges and locking bars. Fictional service vehicles have cut wheel arches, round tyres/rims, panel seams, separate glazing, lights and bumpers; the wreck has rust panels, exposed components and a missing wheel. Garage/marshal buildings have framed openings, roof edges, gutters and roller-door detail; the grandstand has individual seats, an aisle, stairs and support/rail construction. Tyre stacks have rounded carcasses, open beads, shoulder ribs and tread cuts. Jumps and speed bumps retain their existing profiles with cosmetic seams, reinforcement and fixings. Oil and the legacy puddle have restrained broken liquid sheen.

All 37 menu variants use these same shipping model builders. The icons now have 384x232 one-shot studio renders, tuned framing for tall and flat objects, warm key/cool fill lighting, soft contact shading and mipmapped baked textures. The existing 104x82 controls, actions and captions remain. The legacy dirt and pond stamps were also audited: they are canonical terrain/surface stamps, not independent models, and retain the established biome surface presentation. No new placeable kind, dependency, external art, brand, runtime API or player-facing explanation was added. All geometry and the preview shader are original project-authored assets.

Authored vertex colours are treated as sRGB in the prop materials, including snow-covered props. The hazard shader converts those pigments for Mobile's linear lighting while keeping Compatibility's sRGB output. This fixes the washed-out Mobile rubber/foliage/oil revealed by the first comparison. Road/terrain material colour handling is outside this change. Local unindexed model sources are cached (96 entries; pine colour variants 32) and indexed after assembly; thumbnails keep their existing 48-image limit and release the 3D viewport after baking. The track presentation revision was advanced for cache invalidation; concurrent road presentation revisions are preserved separately in the scoped checkpoint.

Runtime files: `game/track/creator_prop_visual.gd`, new `prop_geometry.gd`, `prop_nature.gd`, `prop_equipment.gd`, `prop_structures.gd` (+UIDs), the object/tyre/material sections of `native_track_visual.gd`, `hazard_visual.gd`, `hazard.gdshader`, the pigment setting in `winter_scenery.gd`, `game/editor/object_thumbnail.gd`, and new `prop_preview_shadow.gdshader` (+UID).
