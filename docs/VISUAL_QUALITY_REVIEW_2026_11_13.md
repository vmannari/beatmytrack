# Beat my track visual quality review

13 November 2026. Review only, covering the current racing presentation, vehicle fleet, Garage and menu UI. Requirements: the owner's AAA visual review request; existing V02/V04 material and contour direction, V05 shared scenery and R01 readability constraints.

**Assessment:** the game has a recognizable identity and comparatively polished menus, but the sampled racing environments and vehicle presentation do not yet form a consistent AAA finish. The largest opportunity is coordinated art direction across scenery, materials, lighting and screen readability. More vehicle polygons alone will not close that gap.

The quality target remains the approved mature, restrained, stylized overhead racer: believable proportions, natural colours, clear silhouettes and stable motion. Preserve the accepted camera, physical scale, vehicle identities and opaque-window requirements. The [art direction](../Android_racing_game_blueprint_v2/ui/UI_Art_Direction.md) already calls for one finished car and corner to establish the bar before expanding it.

**Implementation constraints that affect the plan**

The Garage already has key/fill/rim lights, a procedural reflection sky and planar car reflections. Road and terrain shaders already include wear, relief, roughness and filtering; contact shading also exists. Improve their coordination and tuning. Relevant entry points are [Garage environment](../game/ui/garage_environment.gd), [studio sky](../game/ui/studio_sky.gdshader), [floor material](../game/ui/garage_deck.gdshader), [biome lighting](../game/track/track_theme.gd), [surface detail](../game/track/surface_detail.gdshaderinc) and [nature props](../game/track/prop_nature.gd).

The mobile race world is capped at 1.5 million pixels in [game.gd](../game/core/game.gd), lines 1131–1152. At a 3000×2120 drawing surface that implies at most approximately 48.6% linear resolution, or 1457×1030, even if 100% is selected. This is a calculation from current policy, not a device measurement. The Garage separately uses 4× MSAA and full-scale rendering within its viewport size cap ([car_showroom.gd](../game/ui/car_showroom.gd), lines 93–98 and 169–173).

Mobile Adreno/Compatibility deliberately uses custom FXAA with MSAA disabled to protect depth-dependent effects ([graphics preferences](../game/presentation/graphics_preferences.gd), lines 30–37). Preserve that correctness while evaluating sharper quality tiers. A desktop Garage screenshot does not qualify tablet racing quality.

A renderer migration requires its own comparison. Godot's current matrix supports baked lightmaps and reflection probes in Compatibility, while several advanced effects remain renderer-specific. Choose additions against a visible need and measured device cost. [Official renderer comparison](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/rendering/renderers.html).

**Recommended next art milestone**

Use Compact and one Pine corner as the first complete visual slice. Improve the tree family, grass/road hierarchy, selected surface transitions, car materials and grounding together. Carry the same car into the Garage, then check a Night view to establish minimum readability. Preserve the simulation, route boundaries and accepted camera.

Compare fixed before/after views with light and dark paint, ordinary and close Garage views, and the actual race framing. Then record a full-speed lap, a Garage rotation and a Garage-to-race transition under default and proposed quality settings. At the chosen device refresh target, inspect p95/p99 frame times, first-use stalls and sustained thermal behaviour. A proposed 60 Hz target gives 16.67 ms per frame; it is not a measured result here. Once this slice is visually accepted, apply its material and scenery rules across the fleet and remaining biomes.
