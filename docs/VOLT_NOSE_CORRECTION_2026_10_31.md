# Volt nose correction — 2026-10-31

The front edge and fascia were reshaped after the previous nose was rejected as a duck's bill. In the procedural authoring coordinates, the center-to-corner projection fell from 0.30 m to 0.18 m, and the bottom fascia setback from the most forward fascia point fell from about 0.149 m to 0.044 m. The bumper remains gently curved. Fitted normals reduce the pre-existing front-corner crease. The reference body, cowl, windows, wheels, handling and livery interfaces remain in place.

The final source was baked into `electric_reference_smooth_nose_v1`; Electric shadows, six Garage templates and the Electric picture assets were regenerated. An independent same-process comparison found zero surface-array differences between the procedural and prepared versions across 453 mesh instances. The new actual-mesh nose test passes 10/10 and rejects the task-start profile; the retained redesign test passes 149/149 procedural and 150/150 prepared, and the door profile passes 100/100 in both. A seam diagnostic passes 34/34 with a maximum 0.809 mm front-to-side gap in the simplified model. Adapted light visibility checks pass 16/16, and the actual-kit Garage template check passes 24/24. The actual Garage/race check passes 58/58, though its test run logged several frame hitches; this is not a performance measurement.

The front is still simpler and more upright than the reference; the broader vehicle is not yet a close match.
