# Winter construction and breakable decorations

Owner request, 2026-10-16: add igloos, snowmen, snow castles, snow lanterns and ice lanterns to Winter. Snowmen and both lanterns must break with material-appropriate effects. Continue the original native-model quality target established for the themed objects.

Requirements: WC01 — five original Winter objects and actual-model icons; WC02 — snow/powder breakup versus ice shards and extinguished lanterns; WC03 — deterministic contacts, saved effects, replay/rewind/reset; WC04 — bounded prepared geometry/effects and preserved existing behavior; WC05 — native both-renderer evidence and honest device acceptance.

In Create → Winter → Objects, Buildings contains Igloo and Snow castle; Props contains Snowman, Snow lantern and Ice lantern. Winter's existing Populate command includes them with its original road/start clearance, minimum separation, deterministic seeds and 96-object authoring limit. Existing tracks are not automatically rewritten or decorated.

The original kit has a curved block-built igloo with a hollow inner shell, real barrel-vault entry and interior floor; a four-tower snow castle with rounded packed-snow towers, open courtyard, block walls and crenellations; a three-part hand-packed snowman with fitted coal face/buttons, carrot, twig arms, wrapped scarf, rolled hat brim and snow-dusted crown; a hollow snowball lantern; and a thick-walled hollow translucent ice vessel with rounded rim and trapped-air seams. Both lanterns contain a real native candle/flame mesh. Snow and cloth retain restrained opaque shading; the ice shell has filtered coverage and a reflective rim. The warm flame is emissive geometry without adding per-object lights, flicker processes or permanent viewports. All artwork, geometry and shaders are original project-authored work, with no third-party image/model, external generation or runtime dependency.

Snowmen break at a closing speed of 2 m/s; lanterns at 1.6 m/s. Gentler contacts remain forgiving movable nudges. One actual impact produces snow chunks and powder for snow objects, or shards and a smaller icy powder burst for ice lanterns. The intact model, including its flame, disappears on destruction. Chunks briefly loft/tumble and settle, powder expands/fades, and all new debris retires within three seconds of simulation time. Snow/ice/powder fragments are cosmetic material: they cannot alter tyre grip or collide with the car. Existing physical wood and harmless hay keep their previous behavior exactly.

The new effect rows use the existing twelve-field fragment shape; type-specific fields 10/11 store total/remaining lifetime. State snapshot/recorder schema stays at 24 fields/schema 12. Fixed 120 Hz stepping owns contact/damage/lifetime, while presentation derives the transient lift, tumble and fade from the saved values. Pause, rewind and reset therefore restore the flame and every fragment consistently, without wall-clock timing or scene physics. The Winter pool holds at most 192 rows across snow/ice/powder; oldest Winter rows can be replaced, physical wood and hay are retained. Three shared Winter MultiMesh batches render the effects. The old two batches remain unchanged on tracks without Winter breakables.

All five default models remain under 10,000 triangles: Igloo 3,078; Snowman 3,480; Snow castle 9,588; Snow lantern 6,116; Ice lantern 1,332. Existing 96-entry/32 MiB model, 48-image/24 MiB thumbnail, and 12-entry damage-mesh limits are retained. Static snow construction uses its own opaque material batch; retained editor grouping keeps that material separate from ordinary props and the Egyptian stone. Existing car/camera, road/snow/surface artwork, audio assets, local car condition and driving tuning are unchanged by this task.

Physics is pinned to `arcade-planar-0.60` for the added deterministic destruction behavior. Compiler 27, five ordinary laps, rules, input replay format and existing snapshot shape remain. The creator schema enum gains the five Winter IDs and is reconciled with the already-shipping five Egyptian IDs. The local verifier fixture changes only the measured physics manifest pin; its document, recorded inputs and timing remain exact. See [canonical-manifest.json](evidence/winter-craft-2026-10-16/canonical-manifest.json). Matching client/verifier deployment remains a separate step; no service was deployed or live track modified.
