# Ground-bound wind powder — 2026-10-13

The owner rejects wind powder that feels detached from the ground and glued over the scene. `winter-wind-12` replaces the main floating sheets in actual Winter races with moving powder in the opaque road, painted terrain, bridge deck and snowbank-toe materials. Owner visual acceptance remains open.

Requirements and files:

- WIND10/V04: new `game/track/winter_ground_snow.gdshaderinc`, included through `surface_detail.gdshaderinc`, uses the existing spatially varying world wind field and original packed flow artwork. Two reads separate soft powder packets from fine, nearly isotropic grains. Coverage changes the surface pigment, roughness, specular response and road normal/reflection contribution. It has no quad UV boundary, height gap, screen projection or alpha sorting. `creator_road.gdshaderinc`, `terrain.gdshaderinc`, `bridge_road.gdshaderinc` and `winter_bank.gdshader` apply it to their actual surfaces. Bank crests retain their existing sculpted finish; powder concentrates at the toe. The original bank branch form and eight-kind terrain fallback remain.
- WIND11/V05: `winter_air_snow.gdshader` lowers the fixed 48 grains to approximately 2.6–9.8 cm above the flat road, retaining the prior uneven flow and individual travel rates. Real-car footprint protection supplements the existing opaque-depth guard. Surface powder similarly diverts softly around that footprint. The car position is independent of the snow volume's camera-follow focus.
- WIND12/Q01: `winter_snow_wind.gd` discovers supported opaque surfaces after the prepared-world build completes. It installs one run-owned material copy per source material, shares copies across geometry chunks, updates visual uniforms and restores exact original material identities on removal/reconfiguration. A stable-child-count check avoids recurring scene traversal after building. Hidden weather disables surface coverage, pause/background stop clocks and retry clears history. In actual races, both 49 ground cards and 36 bank cards submit zero instances; only the 48-grain batch remains. Existing cards remain a bounded fallback for visuals without surface support. No per-frame CPU particle simulation or new asset.
- G01/R01: `game/core/game.gd` supplies the actual car and binds it after startup creation, since weather is constructed first. `surface_shader_variants.gd` expands the new includes and aliases the inactive sampler outside Winter, retaining fixed role compilation. `native_track_visual.gd` exposes completed-world readiness and uses visual cache revision191 to invalidate older serialized shader bodies. Camera, canonical driving/scoring, audio, vehicle bank-impact clouds and underlying artwork are unchanged by this task.

The native file, terrain shader and material compiler also received concurrent spatial partition, uniform-region and editor-road-preparation work. Those changes are preserved and excluded from this task's source patch using reconstructed concurrent baselines. They affect total scene drawing counts; their benefits are not attributed to wind snow here.
