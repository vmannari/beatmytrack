All fourteen choices remain in each category. Existing item IDs, ownership and saved selections are retained. These desktop captures do not certify phone performance or owner appearance acceptance.

What changed
  • Whole formed engine lids replace the stacked add-ons. Vents are recessed into the panel, with hinges, a gasket and a flush latch.
  • Dished steel and cast wheel centres replace the stick spokes. Real apertures, rolled flanges, barrels and bolted hubs sit inside the tyre envelope.
  • Rear bumpers replace the factory part and attach through mounting shoes. The fence-like guards and unsupported rollers are gone.
  • Cabin frames use continuous bent portals, fitted feet, supported fixtures and practical roof structures. Decorative racks and crosses across the rear view are gone.
