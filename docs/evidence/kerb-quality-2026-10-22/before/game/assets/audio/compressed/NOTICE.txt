Compressed playback derivatives of the existing local Beat my track PCM masters.
The original audio masters remain unchanged in their original directories.
Source receipts and author/licence notices accompany those masters; the four
owner-selected ambience source licences were documented on 2026-10-16.
Compression does not grant new rights.
See manifest.json for each source hash, encoded hash and decoded quality results.

Build-only tools: local FFmpeg 7.0.2/libvorbis, NumPy, SciPy and SoundFile.
Command: scripts/optimize_media_assets.py encode (existing WSL music Python).
No runtime tool, AI service or additional native codec dependency is added.

For rain, thunder/rumble, Desert wind and Winter wind, see
../ambience/NOTICE.txt and ../ambience/source_licenses.json for the named
creators, source links, Pixabay Content License and additional thunder CC0
origin. Commercial incorporation and editing are permitted under the recorded
terms; standalone distribution and claiming ownership of originals are excluded.
The dated evidence is retained in the repository at
docs/evidence/audio-license-clearance-2026-10-16/report.json.
