Changes: mono 44.1 kHz resampling, filtering, loop crossfade, loudness mastering
and 16-bit dither. Attribution applies to tire.wav and audio/video containing it.

All recorded masters are edited for fictional vehicles. No manufacturer
affiliation or claim of exact engine reproduction. Engine variants use cuts,
transposition, equalization, on-load/coast filtering and crossfaded loops.
Body impacts use edited vehicle body/door recordings, not crash recordings.
Desert sand spray is filtered Nox dirty-ground driving foley. grit_0/1/2 use
Nox Iceland black-pebble footstep recordings, filtered/cut/mastered into short
stone-scatter bursts. These are designed gravel foley, not exact car-rock recordings.
Wind and water noise beds are original offline sound design.
Source/derivative filenames and SHA-256 values: manifest.json.
All sources are local build inputs. The game makes no audio-generation/API calls.
