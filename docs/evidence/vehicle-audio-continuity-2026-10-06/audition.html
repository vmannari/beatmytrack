Continuous engine review

Corrected recordings from the actual Godot engine bus. Each twelve-second preview contains three seconds of idle, a smooth acceleration, six seconds of sustained revs across repeated loops, lift-off, then pause. Tire and impact sounds are excluded so the engine is easy to judge.

The resampling bug that inserted repeating silent gaps has been fixed. All 36 looped assets and all 15 captured idle/rev/lift-off segments pass continuity checks. The kart uses one gear. These are authored fictional engine voices, processed from cleared recordings. Hearing quality on the phone remains to be reviewed.
