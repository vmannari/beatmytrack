# Volt and Challenger panel contours

13 November 2026. Scoped source work for VQR04. Native immutable Challenger exterior meshes were not rewritten. The parent task owns the subsequent prepared assets, catalogue images and combined material validation.

## Measured causes and changes

- Volt's original fitted door ribbons had seven triangle centres below their actual rendered backing, out of 1,152 door triangles in the reduced model. The deepest penetration was 2.0069 mm. Endpoint fitting alone did not prevent a chord from crossing a crease. Adaptive fitting keeps the original 0.8 mm endpoint offset, adds 96 reduced door triangles, and leaves no tested triangle centre buried. Minimum reduced door clearance is 0.3111 mm. Bonnet and handle triangle counts are unchanged. See `audit.log` and `refit-audit.log`.
- The physical 1.6–2.8 mm lines also become narrower than a pixel. In the same native camera and lighting, ordinary raster sampling produces alternating dark/empty pixels. `panel_contour.gdshader` integrates physical ribbon width over a pixel, with a small screen-space raster support. World-space centre lines and physical widths are retained. It keeps depth testing, disables depth writes and casts no artificial contour shadow. No global AA setting changes.
- Challenger door, handle and fuel contour positions and index prefix remain exactly those of its native marking surface. Coverage attributes are added without changing their triangle count. Resource-keyed caches retain their keys and have explicit bounds (128 contour meshes, 16 colours; the Challenger assembly cache is limited to four).
- Challenger's bonnet seam is a separate native paint boundary. Hiding the marking surface, hiding dark fascia, disabling shadows and changing culling did not remove its sampling pattern. Moving the bonnet by 1–2 mm changed the join without reliably cleaning it; those diagnostic translations were not shipped. An 8× MSAA diagnostic smoothed the seam, confirming a sampling contribution.
- A 100-segment flush ribbon now follows the native bonnet outer boundary, with the same physical 2.5 mm width and 1.8 mm offset used by Challenger's existing markings. Its edges are fitted to the upper of the existing bonnet/body skins. It joins the existing marking mesh and material, adding exactly 200 triangles and no new mesh instance. Total assembled geometry is **49,968 reduced / 84,924 full**, within the existing strict 50k/85k gates.

The bonnet outline is derived from `simple_hood_paint.res`, whose SHA-256 is embedded in `bonnet_contour.json`. The outer projection is star-shaped around `(x=0.005,z=-1.4)`; the farthest boundary intersection at every native vertex angle excludes internal vent/hole edges. A three-dimensional Douglas–Peucker reduction retains 100 points with maximum measured chord error **0.873736 mm**. The original hood/body paint arrays remain untouched. The regression checks source provenance, retained plan coordinates and actual centroid clearance above both native skins at both detail tiers.

## Final validation and native views

- `geometry-final.log`: **172 checks, 0 failures**, procedural Electric and both native Challenger detail tiers.
- `bonnet-opacity-gl.log` and `bonnet-opacity-mobile.log`: **29 checks, 0 failures each**, including exact bright-cabin-probe invisibility, one unshadowed contour surface, wheel contacts and full assembled triangle budgets.
- `bonnet-candidate3.log` and `bonnet-compare-mobile.log`: same-process paired physical-width controls and integrated shader views for both cars, front and side. Both renderers exit 0 with no engine errors.

Inspect `gl_compatibility-electric-side-sampled.png` / `gl_compatibility-electric-side-integrated.png`, and `gl_compatibility-super_rally-front-sampled.png` / `gl_compatibility-super_rally-front-integrated.png`. Corresponding `mobile-*` files cover Vulkan Mobile. These controls use the final geometry and identical materials except the contour coverage method; they are not full task-start material comparisons. `opaque-final/` contains the final two-tier opacity views and machine-readable acceptance counts.

The native tests used the pinned official Godot 4.7.2 engine, RTX 3090, isolated disposable APPDATA/LOCALAPPDATA and empty backend configuration. Commands, durations and exit codes are in the adjacent `*-receipt.json` files. Exact-byte duplicate PNGs, including unchanged cabin-probe images, were consolidated; `image-deduplication.json` maps every removed duplicate to its retained identical file and hash. Tests do not establish device performance or dynamic shimmer. Fine native edge sampling can remain at some headings; the work substantially improves continuity without claiming that every alias is eliminated.

## Retained diagnostic findings

Initial generic edge pairing failed on imported duplicate normal vertices; the final helper welds only the topology lookup and preserves render positions/normals. An unbatched showroom diagnostic initially failed its self type and was replaced with an independent unbatched car. The first bonnet assembly draft retained shader-unused UV2 data of the original length; that draft was rejected, and the assembly now removes unused UV2/colour channels before adding the surface. Logs remain adjacent to the clean final runs. The initial broad Challenger ray audit includes intentionally concealed parts behind other body sections and is not a claim that the native Challenger marking surface was geometrically refitted.

Production changes: `electric_reference_mesh.gd`, the contour branch of `super_rally_model.gd`, new `panel_contour.gd` / `.gdshader`, new `challenger_panel_contours.gd`, and `bonnet_contour.json`. Test changes are `test_panel_contours.gd` and the original Challenger opacity test's material recognition. Electric fitted cache keys are versioned to `fitted_v2`; the parent task refreshes those native prepared resources and all affected bases/kits/previews. No Blender regeneration, physics, camera, opaque-window, interior or raised-handle change was made.
