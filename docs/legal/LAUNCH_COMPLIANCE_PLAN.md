## Protection against disputes

A blanket “all bugs accepted, no responsibility for anything” clause would not provide the requested protection for Finnish/EU consumer distribution. Unfair terms and mandatory digital-content remedies remain applicable. Calling a build free, beta or early access does not by itself remove those duties; relevant consumer rules can also apply where the consumer supplies personal data for purposes beyond supplying the service. [KKV unfair terms](https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/contracts/unfair-contract-terms/), [digital content](https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/digital-content-and-services/).

The draft limits promises about uninterrupted availability, absence of bugs, universal compatibility, cheat prevention and competitive outcomes. It also gives specific grounds for updates, moderation and discontinuation, and limits liability for appropriate categories of loss where lawful. It preserves mandatory remedies and avoids an arbitrary monetary cap, consumer indemnity, exclusive Finnish court clause, forced arbitration or automatic forfeiture of all paid benefits after a ban. Those additions could create unfairness rather than dependable protection.

Disclose a particular known limitation before acquisition or purchase. If it is a departure from objective conformity requirements that the law permits the player to accept, obtain express separate acceptance. A general terms checkbox or a long list of unspecified bugs is insufficient. Article 8(5), change rules in Article 19 and mandatory effect in Article 22 of [Directive 2019/770](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/770/oj/eng) frame this review; Finnish implementing law and the actual transaction control the result.

Keep the accepted terms version, purchase description, provider transaction and support decision so the business can explain what was agreed and delivered. Collect only the evidence needed under an approved retention policy; a desire to defend disputes is not a licence for indefinite personal-data retention.

## Online service discontinuation

The publisher requires the ability to end online operation and has now chosen operational/commercial wording instead of a consumer-facing list of personal reasons. Section 14 identifies legitimate commercial unsustainability, unsupportable operating/maintenance costs and inability to continue necessary arrangements, subject to existing obligations and mandatory remedies. This confirms a draft policy, not a request to shut down a service.

Make that limit visible before acquisition and purchase, particularly beside permanent ad-free and publishing-capacity offers. Do not market a permanent entitlement as guaranteed lifetime hosting or silently narrow a benefit already sold. Obtain substantive review of the proposed wording and record how existing purchases will be honoured or remedied. A general shutdown reservation does not remove required notice or mandatory consumer remedies. [KKV unfair terms](https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/contracts/unfair-contract-terms/).

Prepare a shutdown procedure covering advance notice and the effective date, stopping sales of benefits that cannot be supplied, outstanding orders and paid entitlements, available local play, required content retrieval, personal-data cleanup/retention and a remaining complaints contact. No fixed notice period is invented in this draft; assess a sufficient period against the actual product commitments and applicable law. Mandatory update obligations must also be assessed when maintenance ends. [KKV digital-content obligations](https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/digital-content-and-services/).

## Sale succession and major gameplay changes

The publisher also requires the ability to sell or license the game and transferable IP, appoint a different maintainer/developer/operator, and make substantial gameplay changes. Section 19 reserves the relevant business/IP rights while distinguishing them from assignment of player contracts. Section 7 permits the limited published-content licence to pass with the game business for the same purposes; it does not claim ownership of players' tracks or rights the publisher does not hold.

An IP sale, subcontracting arrangement and transfer of a player contract are legally different actions. Audit ownership and third-party licences before a transaction, identify which obligations the successor takes on, and obtain any legally required notice/agreement. Do not assume a buyer automatically inherits every asset licence, provider account, consent or permission, or that a sale releases the seller from existing claims. The unfair-terms rules specifically identify transfers that can reduce consumer guarantees without agreement as a potential unfair term. [Directive 93/13 Annex 1(p)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A31993L0013).

Plan any data handover separately: controller/processor roles, lawful basis, necessary records, secure migration, applicable cross-border safeguards, privacy notices and preservation of deletion/consent/ownership restrictions. The general terms cannot turn acceptance into blanket permission for unrelated data uses. [Commission controller and processor guidance](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/application-gdpr_en).

Section 13 now expressly includes substantial changes to handling, controls/cameras, tracks, rules/formats, scoring/rankings, progression/rewards, presentation, editor and online features. It specifies development, balance, feedback, security, compatibility and evidenced sustainability reasons. Assess each actual change against its reason, what customers were promised, paid rights and the notice/termination requirements. A substantial negative impact can trigger statutory remedies even when the change clause exists. Preserve an unchanged conforming service where that is the lawful route chosen, or provide the applicable termination/remedy route. [KKV modification rules](https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/digital-content-and-services/).

This instruction authorises drafting these future contractual provisions. It does not change the current runtime, canonical driving contract, product invariants or existing player agreements, and does not authorise an actual sale, transfer of records or deployment. A successor must review the applicable contracts rather than treating this draft as a completed assignment.

## 2 Finalise the legal package

Have a Finnish adviser review the substantive liability clause, digital-content purchase classifications, withdrawal flow, retained historical identities/content, moderation/DSA classification, and proposed product descriptions. This is a recommended project release gate, not a claim that every game legally requires lawyer certification.

Publish matching terms, privacy notice, community rules, legal contacts, deletion resource and purchase information only after the choices and actual behaviour agree. Preserve an immutable readable copy of each effective terms version. The official legal route should permit reading and saving without sign-in and be accessible from the app and listing. Do not send players to a draft with unresolved placeholders.

The existing privacy notice needs the actual controller, processing purposes and legal bases, recipients/roles, relevant international-transfer safeguards, rights/complaint information, and retention matching the real infrastructure. Maintain the processing inventory, applicable processor agreements and a rights-request procedure. Access, correction, portability where applicable, objection/restriction and erasure need working operational handling; they do not all require new self-service screens. The general response deadline is one month, with the limited extension and notification rules. [Commission obligations](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-business-and-organisations/obligations_en), [EDPB practical guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/find-practical-info/faq_en).

Determine whether retained account IDs, track titles, publication history and scores remain reasonably linkable to people. “Removed account” is a display rule, not proof of anonymisation. Document a lawful basis and applicable erasure exceptions for any retained personal data, or change the retention/redaction implementation. Rehearse cleanup and restoration using synthetic data and the independent erasure journal.

## 3 Complete the legal and privacy engineering

Use existing privacy/community services and platform boundaries. Implement a versioned general-terms record distinct from the current community-rules acceptance. Preserve the exact version/hash, locale, acceptance time and account binding with minimal evidence. Offer a readable/saveable copy before agreement, before paid checkout and at relevant account/publication boundaries. An unchecked explicit action must not double as optional analytics or ad consent. Terms changes must respect existing purchases and any notice/termination requirements; do not silently invalidate old purchases or receipts.

Retain local/offline play and its existing recovery semantics while any general licence acceptance is presented at an appropriate boundary. A pending network response must not delete saves, purchases or authored files. Terms refusal and account deletion are distinct choices. Migration policy must explicitly cover existing accounts and historical purchases rather than invent consent.

Complete the Google-certified CMP/UMP integration for the chosen production advertising modes, privacy options, withdrawal and audience signals. Keep first-party optional analytics off without its own choice. Check the release build's actual SDK network behaviour, including before any consent. Advertising refusal must use only legally and provider-permitted modes; “non-personalised” is not an automatic exemption from storage consent. [AdMob CMP requirements](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/13554116), [UMP guide](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy).

## 4 Finish purchase compliance

The code currently has three publishing-capacity packs, one calendar month ad-free, and permanent ad-free. Production billing is unavailable; current EUR prices are simulator/catalogue values, not proof of final localised checkout prices. The older resume-pack proposal is not this catalogue. See [commerce source](../../backend/bmt_backend/commerce.py) and [Store implementation](../../game/ui/store_page.gd).

Implement the selected Play-compliant billing route and real product details; account-bound verification; durable delivery and acknowledgement; pending/cancel recovery; provider notifications/reconciliation; refunds and reversals. Test both installed entitlement state and server state through retries, account switches and process death. [Play payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738), [billing security](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/security).

Map seller/provider responsibilities per SKU. Establish the applicable digital-content or digital-service withdrawal rules, any valid immediate-supply consent/acknowledgement and durable confirmation, and the 2026 online withdrawal function where withdrawal rights remain. Do not assume a Play refund button fulfils every publisher duty. Defect remedies are separate from a change-of-mind withdrawal. [KKV withdrawal guidance](https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/online-stores/right-of-withdrawal-in-e-commerce/).

Inspect the actual first paid checkout and confirmation in provider sandbox before accepting the flow. Preserve the selected account, product scope, total price and legal version with the purchase without exposing raw provider tokens or collecting unnecessary personal information. Support staff need a safe order-reference lookup, recovery/refund process and decision record.

## 5 Make moderation and contact channels usable

Activate named staff/MFA access and confirm a real moderation operator and backup coverage before public sharing. Existing report/block/appeal and publishing-rule acceptance are useful controls; the latest recorded cloud release has Studio disabled, so source implementation is not operational acceptance. [Public-content implementation](../PUBLIC_CONTENT_SAFETY.md), [cloud receipt](../SECURITY_DEPLOYMENT_2026_10_24.md).

Assess DSA hosting/online-platform status and enterprise-size exemptions. Public track storage/sharing is a strong reason to perform this assessment. Basic hosting duties can remain even when small-business exemptions remove some additional platform/transparency obligations. Provide an easy public illegal-content notice mechanism that does not require a game account; receipt/decision notifications where applicable; reasons for restrictions; relevant review/redress information; user/authority contact points; and escalation for qualifying criminal threats. Audit notification and review against the actual restrictions, not just a generic appeal button. [Traficom hosting obligations](https://www.traficom.fi/en/data-economy-and-data-intermediation/hosting-services).

Do not promise immunity from illegal user content merely because the terms prohibit it. Respond appropriately to notices and binding orders, preserve only necessary evidence, and apply proportionate restrictions. Do not promise automatic permanent loss of all purchased benefits after any reported violation.

The publisher requires explicit temporary/permanent ban powers for offensive racing names, track names/content and other serious misconduct. Section 11 now enumerates the covered surfaces and prohibited behaviour, allows name/content changes through permanent account termination, and explains immediate action for serious or repeated misconduct. Paid access does not exempt a player from enforcement. This is a draft contractual policy; it does not ban any existing account or change current enforcement code.

The requested blanket removal of complaint and paid-content refund rights is not adopted. Sections 9 and 11 instead decline discretionary goodwill refunds solely because a proportionate, justified sanction for the player's material breach prevents account-dependent access. Statutory, contractual and applicable store refund/redress rights remain. Assess unused paid benefits individually, preserve relevant evidence, provide required reasons and review information, and correct mistaken or disproportionate actions. Good-faith complaints, refund requests and criticism must not themselves become ban grounds. [KKV unfair terms and legal remedies](https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/contracts/unfair-contract-terms/), [Traficom restriction reasons and redress](https://www.traficom.fi/en/data-economy-and-data-intermediation/hosting-services).

Build a signed AAB with current target/native-library compatibility and check the actual exported manifest, SDKs and licences. [Target API policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11926878), [developer verification](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10841920), [testing access](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465).

Prepare an incident process for legally required personal-data breach assessment/notification and assess Cyber Resilience Act scope. CRA reporting began on 11 September 2026, while its main requirements apply from 11 December 2027; an in-scope exploited vulnerability or severe incident may require an early warning within 24 hours. Do not treat every software bug as a reportable event. Check the e-commerce accessibility scope and any microenterprise service exemption against the actual publisher. [CRA timeline](https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/safer-and-more-secure-digital-products-2026-09-11_en), [Finnish accessibility scope](https://www.saavutettavuusvaatimukset.fi/en/requirements-act-provision-digital-services/scope-does-act-apply-us).

[Commission closure notice](https://consumer-redress.ec.europa.eu/site-relocation_en), [Finnish Consumer Disputes Board](https://www.kuluttajariita.fi/en/).
