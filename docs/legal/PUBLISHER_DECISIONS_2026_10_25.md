## Annual access and voluntary account deletion

The publisher explicitly selected the interpretation that a deliberately deleted game account's ad-free benefit need not be retained for a replacement account, and selected one year as the duration in any case. Section 8 now offers twelve months, one payment and no automatic renewal. Section 12 ends remaining account-bound benefits on informed voluntary deletion, without a replacement grant or discretionary refund solely for that choice. Uninstalling/reinstalling or changing devices while retaining the same account preserves its unexpired benefits. Mandatory consumer claims and applicable store requirements remain.

The checked Google account-deletion policy does not expressly require purchases to be transferred to a new game account, and billing guidance recognises matching a purchase to the expected in-app account. No blanket transfer requirement following deliberate deletion was identified. This is the basis for the selected contractual model, not an authoritative ruling that every forfeiture is enforceable in every launch region. [Play account-deletion rules](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111), [account/eligibility checks](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/security).

Supercell section 4.2 expressly addresses voluntary account closure and denies compensation for unused virtual items, while section 7 preserves statutory consumer rights. That is a relevant publisher practice, not legal proof for Beat my track's precise annual product. [Supercell terms](https://supercell.com/en/terms-of-service/).

Before sales, implement an actual timed product rather than relabel the current `ad_free_forever` non-consumable. Google documents prepaid plans that do not automatically renew and can be topped up. Verify the supported annual plan, account binding, provider expiry, acknowledgement, refunds/revocations and behaviour for repurchase/top-up. Checkout must identify the exact dates and any treatment of already-active monthly access. The permanent flag must not confer new annual grants; review any legitimate legacy permanent grants separately rather than retroactively expiring them. [Google prepaid plans](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/subscriptions#prepaid-plans-integration).

A store record or already-owned response may survive game-account deletion. Handle that state transparently and keep verification, support and mandatory remedies available; it does not itself prove a duty to grant a new account access. Do not falsify store state, use replacement SKUs to bypass ownership checks or demand another payment for a remedy owed. Product/checkout and deletion-warning fairness still need normal regional review, and early operator-initiated withdrawal may require a remedy for unused paid time. [Google billing responses](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/errors), [KKV unfair terms](https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/contracts/unfair-contract-terms/), [digital-content remedies](https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/digital-content-and-services/).

Do not condition account erasure on completing a purchase dispute first. Remove account access and associated data as required, retain only lawfully necessary records under disclosed purposes/periods, and keep the remedy contact available. Store purchase history is distinct from the game's entitlement and erased identity. [Play account-deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111).

Acceptance must cover annual expiry and calendar boundaries; exact-once delivery/top-up; reinstall/device change on the same account retaining the original expiry; app/web whole-account deletion during active or pending access; later signup receiving no inherited benefit; cached devices, refund/revocation and provider ownership state; and backups that do not resurrect erased accounts or grants. LCP-39 implements this distinction and required claims handling, rather than assuming automatic restoration onto a new account. No price, provider configuration or annual entitlement code is implemented by these drafting decisions.

## Track immutability: concrete source gaps

Current publication data already has version identities, but [publication commit](../../backend/bmt_backend/publications.py) can append a new version to the same logical track using its authoring identity. [Creator hide](../../backend/bmt_backend/tracks.py) allows the owner to hide a track while retaining scores/share references. These do not fully implement the selected no-revision/no-withdrawal policy.

Before applying revision 5, prevent creators from replacing/revising/hiding an existing publication through native UI, direct API calls, queued commits or old clients. Keep acknowledged exact retries recoverable without another slot charge; let a deliberately separate new publication have a separate track identity and charge. Preserve existing historical references and lawful moderation/privacy/legal handling. Do not reinterpret a compatibility derivative as an overwrite of an old race's layout. Document legacy handling; these drafting changes do not revoke existing contractual rights or silently alter old acceptances.

Proposed publication confirmation:

> **Publish this track?**
>
> Publishing uses one slot. After publication you cannot edit, replace, hide or delete this track. It may remain available after you delete your account so races, rivalries and challenges can keep using it. A changed layout must be a separate publication using another slot. Section 7 permits the game's operation, development, separately versioned adaptations and promotion; mandatory legal and privacy rights remain.
>
> **Read terms** · **Publish & use 1 slot** · **Cancel**

This is proposed copy, not a shipped screen or completed agreement.

## Notices, translation and operator coverage

The chosen 60-day in-game notice needs implementation: issue/effective dates, regional/feature scope, persistence through the notice period, a readable retained copy where needed and sales cutoff. A transient toast, changed web text or mutable banner alone does not establish legally required durable notice. Do not collect player email addresses merely to assume a channel the publisher did not select; assess additional required channels on the actual facts. Urgent exceptions need specific reasons and the applicable notice/remedy handling. [KKV notice fairness](https://www.kkv.fi/en/consumer-affairs/contracts/unfair-contract-terms/).

AI can generate translations, but the relevant scope includes long legal documents as well as short game labels. Keep a terminology glossary, versioned source/translated texts and checks for meaning, omissions and purchase/rights consequences. Verify required document delivery and express language choice where applicable; the translation method does not replace that process. [OQLF contract-language guidance](https://www.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/francisation/entreprises/Contrats-adhesion.pdf). Support languages and who checks regional legal accuracy remain open.

A sole publisher can be the initial operator. Confirm available coverage and escalation, use individual secured staff access, and rehearse reports, false-positive review, deletion, purchase complaints and security incidents before launch. The tentative staffing answer does not establish actual availability or a named backup.

No runtime code, rewards, balances, track records, staff access, provider state, translations or notices were changed or published by this task. Mechanical document checks do not validate the chosen purchase-deletion policy or establish launch compliance.
