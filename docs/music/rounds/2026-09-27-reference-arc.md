## Interpretation / proposal, not artistic approval

The desired development has a larger emotional range than the earlier generic
restrained-background-rock brief: solitude → grounded movement → confident
urgency → an earned, immediately recognizable melodic payoff. Background
function means mix discipline and room for driving, not lack of personality.

| Stage | Reference role in this proposal | Musical development |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | Low Roar | Intimate, suspended atmosphere. An original short melodic idea, soft spacious texture, implied pulse; interest from the first phrase. |
| Momentum | CCR; ZZ Top | Add a tangible bass/drum pocket and a compact answering guitar figure. Establish bodily movement while retaining the opening melody. |
| Drive | Peer Gunt; Lynyrd Skynyrd; Molly Hatchet | Broaden into a live band with grit, rhythm-guitar weight and occasional complementary guitar answers. Preserve groove and space. |
| Flow | Green Day; Blondie | Tighter forward articulation, clearer backbeat and concise hooks. Momentum comes from phrase/rhythm changes, not a volume jump. |
| Lift | Muse; Audioslave | Add harmonic tension, a stronger bass/riff relationship and a rising answer that anticipates a return. Avoid filling every register. |
| Breakthrough | The Final Countdown | Transform the opening motif into a confident anthem with a broad melodic lead and satisfying resolution. Bright synth/brass-like colour is an option to test. |

These reference roles express a design reading of the user's list; they are not
claims that each artist belongs exclusively to a stage. Do not combine all names
into one generation prompt or change genres at each intensity threshold.

## Compositional bridge

Propose one short original motif with a recognizable contour and a deliberate
answer. Introduce its restrained form at the start; reveal its complete confident
form at the payoff. Keep a common pulse and harmonic foundation while changing
articulation, register, rhythmic density and phrase endings. A half-time opening
can become a driving backbeat at the same underlying tempo. Groove families
must be chosen coherently: southern shuffle and straight punk drive do not
automatically layer together. No numeric BPM/key is approved yet.

The earlier no-synth/no-distortion reading is too restrictive for this new brief.
Distinguish rejected sounds from a permanent instrument ban. Instrumental remains
the working assumption; no request to add vocals was made.
