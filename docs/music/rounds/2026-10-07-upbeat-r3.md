# Upbeat score correction - round 3

Owner feedback: "The soundtrack is not fit for a racing game, it is too melancholic. It needs to be more upbeat. Also remove the hover on sounds from buttons and other objects."

Baseline: installed original chamber score v1, mix 2, including Open Horizon,
First Light and Workshop. Rejected for mood/game fit; no parts were explicitly
approved. Preserve those recordings and source/provenance for comparison.

Focused correction: one revised upbeat thematic family, retaining the installed
two-colour mapping and five compatible layers. Use major-key cadences, a rising
and answering mid-register motif, shorter piano phrases and a light rhythmic
foundation present from the opening. Later hand-percussion/bowed figures develop
motion; avoid returning to an introspective sustained-string opening. Menu gets
the same brighter identity with a faster, lighter arrangement.

Preserve: existing instrument recordings/licences, comfortable levels, exact
race-loop grid, one-minute build, performance qualification, collision recovery,
rewind/retry/focus lifecycle, car audio and saved preferences.

Change: harmony/melodic phrase direction and rhythmic articulation. Remove all
hover and keyboard-focus sound hooks/cue playback; keep activation/confirm/back.

Hypothesis: the major harmony and purposeful pulse fix the melancholic mood and
give the score forward racing motion without relying on greater loudness.

Success evidence: new playable outputs; exact synchronized loops and combined
headroom/mono checks; actual Godot playback; silent hover/focus with audible
activation; unchanged observer/canonical contracts. Artistic acceptance and
subjective listening remain pending; technical measurements are not listening.
