# Sports rock recompose

Owner: "the soundtrack is still too mellow, it should be more sporty and energetic, we need to compose it again. The instruments should be be like from 80's rock, the ability to build athmosphere from Ori games. But again, remember this is still background music"

Baseline: locked-in band v1, 144 BPM race/112 BPM menu. Rejected for mellow mood;
no prior musical parts were explicitly approved. Preserve all recordings/masters.

New composition: original low muted-guitar riff, sharper bass/kick relationship,
firm acoustic backbeat, short tom fills, brighter driven electric-guitar responses
and wider chorus guitars. Quiet evolving string/analog-style textures establish
depth and anticipation. Four-note identity stays recognizable through the rewrite.
The game references are artistic direction; no songs/audio/conditioning copied.

Keep: background role and car-audio space;144/112 BPM; compatible six-layer clock,
confidence/sector/PB/rival behavior, reserved payoff, controlled silence, stings,
hover silence and persistent levels. No simulation/camera/backend changes.

Hypothesis: riff articulation, harmonic direction and electric-guitar/drum timbre
provide sporting energy that the clean-band version lacks, while atmosphere is
carried by restrained supporting texture instead of an introspective opening.

Evidence: new playable source/game outputs and a matched-loudness old/new group;
loops, mono/headroom, combined layers, real mixer and car masking measures.
Artistic acceptance requires owner listening. No agent subjective listening or
AAA approval is claimed.
