Compressed playback derivatives of the existing local Beat my track PCM masters.
The original masters, source receipts, author/license notices and manifests remain
unchanged in their original directories. Compression does not grant new rights.
See manifest.json for each source hash, encoded hash and decoded quality results.

Build-only tools: local FFmpeg 7.0.2/libvorbis, NumPy, SciPy and SoundFile.
Command: scripts/optimize_media_assets.py encode (existing WSL music Python).
No runtime tool, AI service or additional native codec dependency is added.

The owner-selected recordings retain the rights limitations documented with the
original masters. Distribution rights still require their existing acceptance.
