# Google sign-in button

`google_sign_in_light.png` is Google's unmodified pre-approved sign-in artwork,
downloaded 2026-09-28 from
https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/signin-assets.zip.

Archive member:
`Android + Web/PNG @4x/Light/Theme=Light, Show text=Yes, Shape=Square, Platform=Android+Web@4x.png`.
720×160 pixels; SHA256 `e048546f162b43a5f3809796f88df02a8e13f07ca1280a9c1635018956e05c05`.

Use is restricted to identifying the Google sign-in action under Google's
[Sign in with Google branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines).
Google retains its trademarks. This is not original game artwork or a general
license to reuse Google's branding. The game preserves the button's aspect ratio,
logo, colors and rendered typography. The browser handoff renders Google's own
Identity Services button directly from Google's library.
