# Panama Custom Paint 03

Eighteen original full-colour game-livery atlases generated individually with the built-in image_gen tool for Beat my track on 2026-11-15. Three designs per current car. The active collection excludes portraits, people, faces, animals, masks and skulls; scenery, instruments and machinery are the illustrated subjects.

Two La Estrella bus-paint photographs were supplied as style-only references for vivid airbrushing, shaded nickname lettering and ornamental borders. Their portrait/character subjects were explicitly excluded. Reference photographs are not game assets and are not distributed here. URLs, roles and original generated-file paths are in provenance.json; exact generation prompts are retained in docs/evidence/diablo-liveries-2026-11-15/generation-prompts.json.

Every source PNG is an unchanged copy of its generated original. Shader charts fit artwork to the existing cars. Chrome, candy and metallic effects in the artwork are painted illusions; physical paint material remains the existing game finish. Names are original fictional livery nicknames, without external sponsor marks.

This is a review collection. Owner appearance acceptance, physical-device checks and paid-product integration remain before a commercial release.
