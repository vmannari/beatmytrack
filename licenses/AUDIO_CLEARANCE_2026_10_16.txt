Beat my track - recorded weather ambience licence evidence
Reviewed 2026-10-16. Requirements AL01-AL06, Q01; no audio edits.

Scope and result

Four original recordings have a documented commercial-game use basis under the
Pixabay Content License. This covers the current rain loop, thunder cue and
derived storm rumble, three Desert wind loops, two Winter wind loops and their
seven compressed playback encodings. The current sound and mastering are kept.

Evidence and identity

game/assets/audio/ambience/source_licenses.json is the source registry.
docs/evidence/audio-license-clearance-2026-10-16/report.json records exact
original, edited-master and Ogg hashes, public-source observations, checks and
limits. The five mastering manifests retain all existing edit parameters and
audio hashes, with corrected creator names and source/licence metadata added.

The owner confirms the originals came from legal sources stating free use.
Each preserved MP3 matches the hash recorded when it was edited. Its filename,
asset ID, creator and duration match the public provider listing. The original
download date/account was not supplied and is not invented. No fresh provider
MP3 download or independent remote hash comparison is claimed.

Dated selected primary-source extracts are retained in the evidence sources/
directory. They are labelled text extracts, not raw page archives or provider
certificates. Direct HTTP page archiving returned 403; the public web retrieval
tool supplied the retained observations without bypassing access controls.
Pixabay's FAQ documents retaining links, filenames and the licence summary for
tracks without a supplied Content ID certificate:
https://pixabay.com/service/faq/
No certificate is fabricated and no account or author was contacted.

Distribution and future rebuilding

The Android export explicitly includes the ambience NOTICE.txt and source
registry, alongside the existing compressed-audio notices. The original MP3s
and acquisition evidence remain outside game resources and are not packaged.
See the evidence report for actual archive-inspection results.

scripts/audio_license_records.py supplies the reviewed metadata to all five
mastering builders and rejects a different original hash. A changed recording
requires a new source review; a changed derivative requires refreshed hashes.
The offline check is:
python scripts/check_audio_licenses.py --require-originals
On another checkout without the private original MP3s, omit --require-originals;
the checker still verifies the registry, evidence, edited masters and encodings.

This is the completed source/licence documentation for these four recordings.
Historical "metadata not supplied" statements describe the earlier edit date
and are superseded by this review for the pinned source hashes. Other assets
retain their own source licences and acceptance records. No phone listening,
APK installation, backend change or production deployment was performed.
